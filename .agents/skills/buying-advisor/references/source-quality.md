# Source Quality

Use current, source-backed research before recommending a model.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer:

1. Manufacturer pages for exact specs, variants, warranty, dimensions, release
   details, compatibility, and safety certifications.
2. Retailer pages in the user's market for current price, availability, SKU,
   return policy, and variant names.
3. Independent professional reviews with measurements or repeatable tests.
4. Specialist forums and long-term owner reports for reliability, defects, and
   real-world limitations.
5. General review roundups only as candidate discovery, not decisive evidence.

Avoid relying on:

- Affiliate listicles with no testing details.
- SEO pages that paraphrase manufacturer specs without verification.
- Old reviews when the model has been refreshed or replaced.
- Marketplace listings with unclear seller, variant, warranty, or region.

## Freshness rules

- Treat prices, availability, bundles, and promotions as volatile.
- Check publish/update dates where visible.
- For fast-moving electronics, verify whether a newer model generation exists.
- For appliances, tools, furniture, and safety products, check whether the exact
  model/SKU is still sold in the user's market.

## Evidence to capture

For each serious candidate, capture:

- Exact model name and variant/SKU.
- Current price range and stores checked.
- Key specs tied to user requirements.
- Major strengths and limitations from testing or owner reports.
- Warranty/return constraints if they matter.
- Any known defects, recalls, region locks, subscription requirements, or
  compatibility warnings.

## Confidence labels

High:
- Multiple credible current sources agree.
- Exact model is available in the user's market.
- Hard constraints are satisfied and tradeoffs are clear.

Medium:
- Sources are credible but limited, prices vary materially, or one preference is
  unresolved.

Low:
- Current availability cannot be verified, sources conflict, the market is very
  sparse, or the user declined important clarifying questions.
